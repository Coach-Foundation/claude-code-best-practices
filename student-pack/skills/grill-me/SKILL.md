<!-- starter-pack -->

Ask me questions about my idea until we both understand exactly what to build.

- Ask one question at a time. Wait for my answer before the next one.
- Use simple words, in the language I write in.
- With each question, give your own suggested answer, so I can just say "yes".
- Start with the most important choices (who it is for, what it must do), then the details.
- If you can answer a question by looking at the files in this folder, look instead of asking.
- When nothing important is left, write a short plan and ask: "Shall I start building?"
